# Mate and Chat Profile

**Intent:** Support finding, arranging, and coordinating baseball outings with clear status and low-friction mobile controls.

- Keep filters, date controls, party status, and conversation actions understandable without hover.
- Density 7, motion 2, variance 4 are search dials only. Do not trade touch clarity or message readability for density.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, touch target checks, long Korean messages, and empty/error states.
- Representative harness component: `src/components/MateChatComposerPanel.tsx#MateChatComposerPanel`.
