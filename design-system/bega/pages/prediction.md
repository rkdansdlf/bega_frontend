# Prediction and Statistics Profile

**Intent:** Present baseball predictions and statistics with enough context to interpret comparisons and outcomes.

- Keep labels, legends, values, and error states readable; do not encode status by color alone.
- Density 8, motion 1, variance 3 are search dials only. Pro Max's enterprise-gateway palette and font pairing are unadopted.
- Prefer stable, compact information hierarchy and allow long team, player, and explanatory text to wrap.
- Representative harness component: `src/components/prediction/PredictionMatchesErrorView.tsx#PredictionMatchesErrorView`.
